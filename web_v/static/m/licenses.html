YARA-X
VirusTotal's YARA engine; runs the YARA-Forge rules on the server. BSD 3-Clause License. github.com/VirusTotal/yara-x

Curated YARA rules collected from public rule sets. Each rule keeps its own licence (mostly Detection Rule License 1.1 and CC BY-SA 4.0) and its author, who is credited with every match. github.com/YARAHQ/yara-forge
URLhaus (abuse.ch)

Phishing.Database

gitleaks
