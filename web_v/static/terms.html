1. What CyberScan is

CyberScan is a free tool that performs static analysis of files, links and text: it reads their contents and structure without running them, and explains what it finds in plain words. By using the website or the apps you agree to these terms.

2. No guarantee

A result is an opinion based on evidence, not a guarantee. “Nothing found” means the checks found no indicators; it does not mean a file is safe. A warning does not prove a file is malicious. Static analysis cannot see everything a program might do when it runs, new threats may not match any known pattern, and some checks may not finish in time (the result then says so).

CyberScan is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. Do not rely on it as your only protection; keep your system and antivirus up to date and use your judgement.

3. Acceptable use

  • Only submit files, links and text you have the right to share.
  • Do not use the service to attack, overload or probe it or anyone else, to bypass its limits, or to develop or test malware.
  • Automated or bulk use of the website is not allowed without permission; the desktop app exists for large or repeated scans.
  • Security research on CyberScan itself is welcome under the vulnerability disclosure policy.

4. Repair and quarantine

The repair features rebuild a copy of a file without the parts that do not belong. Always keep the original until you have checked the repaired copy. CyberScan never changes your original file without your action.

5. AI answers

“Ask the AI” answers from a reviewed guide to the project. AI can be wrong; the scan verdict itself always comes from the scanner, not from the AI. A small free number of AI questions is available per day.

6. Liability

To the extent the law allows, the authors of CyberScan are not liable for any loss or damage arising from the use of, or inability to use, the service or its results, including data loss, a missed threat or a false alarm.

7. Changes and contact

These terms may change; the current version is always on this page with its date, and changes are listed in the changelog. How your data is handled is described in the privacy policy. Questions: mahdi-alhajji@zohomail.sa.
